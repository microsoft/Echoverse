Why used vs. CPO?

Used — best price, sold as-is or with limited dealer warranty.
Certified Pre-Owned — manufacturer-inspected, factory-backed extended warranty, slightly higher price.
For an 8-cylinder EchoMotor with 50K+ miles, CPO is often worth the premium for the powertrain warranty alone.
